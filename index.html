About Banned Bible

If you are reading this from a western country, you likely have the distinct privilege of living in a place with broad legal rights and freedoms—a luxury that allows you to freely read and share Scripture with others without looking over your shoulder.

You may not realize that up to 1 in 4 people on earth do not have this same level of freedom.

In closed totalitarian regimes, strict theocracies, and heavily monitored states, the distribution of the Bible is either strictly outlawed or severely restricted. In these environments, the definition of a "crime" is often dangerously broad: simply possessing a copy of the Scripture, sharing a verse digitally, or handing a physical Bible to another person can trigger severe legal penalties, prolonged imprisonment, or death.

We take for granted what others risk everything to hold.

  • Read the Word with fresh eyes and a deep awareness of the cost borne by believers around the globe.
  • Pray on it with a thankful heart for the freedoms you enjoy, and pray earnestly for the Lord to open doors for access and unhindered reading for people living in censored regions.
  • Share it wide and far, holding fast to the truth while it can be freely spoken—and even when it can't.

Not sure where to start reading? We suggest starting with the Gospel of John to get to know Jesus and his teachings, then reading Acts and Romans to continue the story. You can read a Psalm each day for prayer, comfort, or for reflection, and a chapter of Proverbs each day. Take your time, and enjoy noticing what speaks to you.
